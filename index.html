Selected evaluation case studies

The following are self-contained demonstration case studies that illustrate my evaluation method. They are portfolio examples, not claims that these exact deliverables were commissioned by Odin or another client.

Case study 01 · Response quality

Assessing an AI-generated answer

Objective: Determine whether a response answers the user’s question accurately and clearly.

Approach: Identify the key requirements, inspect factual claims, check relevance and completeness, and note unclear or unsupported statements.

Deliverable: A concise quality assessment with evidence-based comments and suggested corrections.

Case study 02 · Fact-checking

Spotting unsupported claims

Objective: Distinguish reliable information from assumptions or invented details.

Approach: Break the answer into checkable claims, verify claims where suitable references are available, and flag uncertainty instead of treating guesses as facts.

Deliverable: A claim-level review highlighting verified points, unsupported statements, and corrections needed.

Case study 03 · Instruction following

Checking compliance with a prompt

Objective: Evaluate whether an AI response follows all parts of a multi-part instruction.

Approach: Convert the prompt into a checklist, compare the response against each requirement, and record omissions, contradictions, or format errors.

Deliverable: A structured assessment of compliance, completeness, and areas for improvement.

Case study 04 · Data quality

Reviewing structured data with Python

Objective: Inspect a dataset for quality issues before analysis.

Approach: Use Python-based processing to examine missing values, inconsistent formats, duplicates, and unusual patterns, then summarize findings.

Deliverable: A concise data-quality summary and recommendations for further review.
